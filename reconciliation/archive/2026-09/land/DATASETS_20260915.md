## Historical sources: recover, do not silently replace

**Original MODIS Collection 6:** NASA retired MCD12Q1.006 on 31 July 2023. Its
catalogued years are 2001–2020; the exact year used by the historical model is
still unconfirmed. The strongest route is to recover the authors' original
granule inventory, files or aggregated class-area table. Collection 6.1 may be
used as a labelled substitution, never as evidence of an exact input replay.
[NASA catalogue record](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/modis-terraaqua-land-cover-type-yearly-l3-global-500m-sin-grid-v006).

**Historical WDPA:** the paper references 2022, but the exact monthly snapshot
has not been recovered. Please retain any original WDPA archive you already
have; otherwise this needs an archive or author enquiry, not another download
of the latest release. Record the month and polygon/point treatment. A present
release filtered by designation year is not equivalent to an old snapshot,
because boundaries and records are revised. Protected Planet publishes monthly
versions and distinguishes public/non-commercial from licensed/commercial data.
[Protected Planet distribution explanation](https://www.protectedplanet.net/en/news-and-stories/why-do-we-have-different-versions-of-the-wdpa).

**ETOPO1:** the upstream Salmon methods identify this 1-arc-minute source.
It remains publicly downloadable from NOAA, so no user account or manual
download is currently needed. It is a historical-method sensitivity; its use
in the exact Verschuur implementation is not established. Record registration:
the authoritative grid/node-registered version differs from the derived,
slightly smoothed cell/pixel-registered version.
[NOAA ETOPO archive](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/etopo-global-relief-model),
[ETOPO1 registration details](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/sites/g/files/anmtlf171/files/2023-01/Product%20Details.pdf).

## Pilot checks and promotion gates

Steps 1–4 are complete for the three centered cells, with refined integration
convergence and separate validated land exports. The full 40-cell/global
expansion and model promotion remain outstanding.

1. Validate native filenames, year/collection, HDF layers, projection, fill
   values and pixel coverage; hash received files without altering them.
2. Reaggregate native classes without exclusions and compare with the existing
   2022 CMG fractions. Quantify class-rounding and edge/resampling effects.
3. Intersect native class pixels with the **joint** slope/protected-area mask
   using physical area weights. Test mask resolution and pixel-boundary rules;
   do not multiply independent marginal exclusion fractions.
4. Produce separate class-by-class area ledgers for raw, slope-only,
   protection-only and joint exclusions. Apply the shipping share once. Keep
   fixed-PV density and tracking sensitivity explicit.
5. Recompute the three-site supply curves with only the accepted land change,
   then expand to the 40-cell diagnostic set before global processing.

Higher resolution improves spatial intersection, but does not establish the
truth of the classification or suitability factors. NASA warns that some
training sites changed, especially after 2021. Treat 2022 as the controlled
comparison year; add an earlier-year and independent land-cover sensitivity
before claiming that the revised global input is more realistic.
[NASA quality notice](https://landweb.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/knownissue?as=61&sat=TerraAqua&sensor=MODIS).

The archived historical supplier surface stays frozen. Reconstructed outputs
remain under `replication/`, and revised-model outputs under `revised/`; shared,
immutable source files do not merge those assumptions or result stacks.
