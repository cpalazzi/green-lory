## 1. Problem and required outcome

The objective is to explain why the ammonia suppliers in green-porpoise change
so much when its historical LCOA/capacity input is replaced, and to produce two
defensible, separate inputs:

1. A reproduction of the stated historical methodology and, as far as the

2. An improved green-lory input whose departures have explicit physical,
   spatial, accounting or modelling reasons.

The primary historical endpoint is **2050 MOD-AMB, RCP4.5, 70% ammonia adoption,
Way cost basis, no subsidies**. RCP4.5/90% is a separate project benchmark.
Matching one rounded cost or a visually similar Australian network is not
sufficient. Do not tune land fractions to obtain the desired map.

dataset to remain unreproduced. The important target is the capacity input
actually responsible for the published shipping network, with a reason for

## 4. What the land reconstruction has established

The quoted method is implemented as a separate source-substituted stack:
classify land within explicit 1° cells; apply protected-area and >15° slope
exclusions jointly; aggregate class-specific suitable areas; apply Table 2
factors; then apply the separate 2% shipping allocation once. Wind uses
200 km²/GW. PV uses approximately 9 km²/GW at the equator with an explicitly
documented latitude adjustment following van de Ven equation 6.

Available data are MODIS **2022 Collection 6.1**, WDPA **February 2026**, and
GEBCO **2025**. They are not the exact historical vintages. Native MCD12Q1 is
nominally 500 m, not the approximately 250 m stated in the paper; MCD12C1
provides class fractions at 0.05°. The historical Collection 6 year, exact
protected-area snapshot, and original terrain processing remain unresolved.

Forty cells were tested with corrected CMG geography under both centered and
southwest cell conventions. All 80 results passed the recorded QA. Native
MCD12Q1 was then integrated for the three focal cells with progressively finer
integration grids. The final convergence difference was below 0.0001%.

## 5. Capacity implementation: the central distinction

For a reference design producing Q₀, define wind footprint as Pwind/5 and PV
footprint as (Pfixed + r × Ptracking)/PV-density, in km². Proportional scaling
under exclusive shared land is:

`Qscaled = Q₀ × min(Awind/Fwind, APV/FPV, Aunion/(Fwind + FPV))`.

Omit a technology ratio when its footprint is zero. Treating wind and solar
as independent budgets drops the union term; that is a different land-sharing
assumption, not a unit correction. The original prose does not resolve every
mixed-technology allocation detail. Both interpretations should be retained
as bounded reconstruction variants until original code is recovered.

Crucially, Qscaled is the largest **copy of that design**, not the site's
maximum ammonia production. Re-optimising the renewable/storage mix at larger
output can give a much larger feasible quantity at a higher cost.

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Atacama | 9.201 | 67.93% | 56.15% | 13.12% |

| Central Australia | 9.186 | 68.04% | 56.25% | 8.43% |

The denominator is primary renewable electricity dispatched to the power bus.
It excludes recycled electricity from fuel-cell and battery links. Available
renewable generation is a different, larger denominator; both efficiencies
are in `energy_efficiency_comparison.csv`. Dispatch data are absent from the
saved legacy-lcoa summary files, so an actual legacy-lcoa efficiency is **not
yet available**. The historical-style green-lory emulation gives approximately
67.86–68.22% HHV, or 56.10–56.40% LHV, on the dispatched basis.

The model uses ammonia HHV **6.25 MWh/t = 22.5 MJ/kg**. For reporting, LHV
**5.1667 MWh/t = 18.6 MJ/kg** makes efficiency 18.6/22.5 = 0.82667 times the HHV
figure for the same physical process. This is a convention change, not a loss
of tonnes or a different plant. Changing an internal heating-value constant
without consistently transforming loads, link efficiencies and storage units
would change the physics incorrectly. Hydrogen's internal HHV basis is
39.4 MWh/t. The shipping deposit uses 18.8 rather than the later project's
18.6 MJ/kg; preserve that distinction in network reproduction.
[Heating-value reference: IRENA, Annex G](https://www.irena.org/-/media/Files/IRENA/Agency/Publication/2022/May/IRENA_Innovation_Outlook_Ammonia_2022.pdf).

Atacama's raw PV profiles occasionally exceed 1.0 (fixed peak 1.0602, tracker
1.1227). Metadata do not establish their AC/DC rating basis. No clipping was
applied. Clipping would remove only 0.13% of annual fixed generation and 1.50%
of tracking generation there, so it cannot by itself explain a factor-of-two
capacity gap. Resolve normalization before calling the profiles physically
validated.

## Bottom line

The corrected stated-paper land method is the right reproduction comparator;
the present smaller land/capacity numbers are not automatically more realistic.
For these cells, native land-cover detail makes almost no difference, while
packing, renewable mix, shared-land semantics and **how plant capacity is
defined** make large differences. Updated green-lory's finite-site optimisation
is more defensible than declaring the scaled cheapest design to be the site's
absolute limit. Exact historical plant/input provenance remains the major
unclosed part of the reconciliation.
